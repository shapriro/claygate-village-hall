Our Flexible Spaces & Facilities

Whether you're holding a dance, wedding reception, children's party, conference, or drama production, we have spaces to suit your needs.

Main Hall

Our largest and most versatile room with high ceilings, polished wood flooring, and direct access to both stage and kitchen facilities.

  • 📏 Size: 15m × 10.5m (approx. 50ft × 35ft)
  • 🪑 Capacity: 120 dinner-dance / 210 theatre lecture
  • 🍽️ Includes 15 large event tables + chairs
Theatrical & AV

Stage & Technical Equipment

Fully equipped proscenium stage with theatrical lighting rig, PA audio system, radiomics, drop-down projector screen, and backstage dressing rooms.

  • 🎭 Proscenium stage: 10.5m × 5.5m (35ft × 18ft)
  • 🔊 Built-in PA with wireless microphones
  • 📽️ High-definition projector and motorized screen
Intimate & Accessible

Small Hall

Ideal for regular fitness and yoga classes, clubs, educational workshops, children's parties (under 11), and community group meetings.

  • 📏 Size: 10.5m × 5m (approx. 35ft × 15ft)
  • 🧘 Warm natural light and parquet flooring
  • 🚪 Direct ground-level accessible entry
Meetings & Admin

Committee Room

Dedicated space designed for formal committee sessions, AGM meetings, board gatherings, and small presentation groups.

  • 👥 Capacity: Up to 20 seated
  • 📶 High-speed guest Wi-Fi coverage
  • ☕ Direct kitchen access for refreshments
Catering Ready

Commercial-Grade Kitchen

Comprehensively equipped catering facility featuring modern induction hob, commercial electric range, high-capacity dishwasher, and plate warmers.

  • ⚡ Fast cycle commercial dishwasher
  • 🧊 Large refrigeration units and serving hatch
