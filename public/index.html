Conveniently Located in Elmbridge, Surrey

Claygate Village Hall is situated on Church Road in the leafy village of Claygate, Esher (KT10 0JP). We are within easy reach of Esher, Hinchley Wood, Thames Ditton, Long Ditton, Oxshott, Cobham, and Kingston upon Thames, with direct rail connections via Claygate Station (South Western Railway to London Waterloo and Guildford).

Whether you are looking for a birthday party venue hire near Esher, a wedding reception hall in Surrey, or regular weekly studio space for dance, fitness, or toddler groups, our flexible facilities and on-site parking make us the premier choice across Elmbridge.
